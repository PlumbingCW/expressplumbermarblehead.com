Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website.

Talk to a plumber
(339) 216-9771
Call Now

Information you give us by phone

When you call, you may share your name, the service address, a callback number and details about the plumbing problem. We use that information to book and carry out the visit.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want to continue on a recorded line, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Website information

This website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed. We use that data to keep the site running and secure.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use this website to collect payment details.

Third-party content

Pages on this site embed Google Maps. Google may set cookies or collect usage data when the map loads. Review Google's own privacy policy for how it handles that data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@expressplumbermarblehead.com or to the address listed in the footer.
