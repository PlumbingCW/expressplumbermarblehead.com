How to book a plumber

Call (339) 216-9771 and tell the dispatcher what is happening, where the property is and whether water is still running. We book by phone only, so there is no form to fill in and no inbox to wait on.

If you can, note the water heater age, where the main shutoff is and any photos you can take safely. Those details help the plumber bring the right parts the first time.

Emergency calls and after hours

The emergency plumber line does not switch to voicemail at night. Weekends and holidays are covered too, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

For a gas smell, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.
