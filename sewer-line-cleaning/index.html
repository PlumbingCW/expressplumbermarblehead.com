Emergency sewer line cleaning in Marblehead, MA

A blocked main sewer line sends wastewater back through the lowest drain in the house, and our line is answered 24/7. Stop using water, keep people and pets away from the affected area and call. Same-day visits are offered when a slot is available.

Sewer line cleaning is the removal of the blockage between your house and the municipal connection. It differs from clearing a single sink, because the line is larger, longer and often runs under the yard or driveway. Roots, grease, wipes and a sagging pipe are the usual causes.

Emergency: sewage backing up into the house

Wastewater carries bacteria, so treat any backup as contaminated. Keep children and pets away, avoid contact and do not run water that adds to the flow.

Sewer lines in an older, rocky town

Marblehead's older neighborhoods have sewer laterals that were laid decades ago, and some pipe sits in or near ledge, which limits how it can be dug up and replaced. A line that clogs repeatedly in one of those areas is worth a camera look before anyone commits to excavation.

The Marblehead Water and Sewer Department handles the public sewer. If you are not sure whether your home is on town sewer or on a private system, ask the department before any work begins.

Cable machines and jetters are used according to the manufacturer's guidelines. A repair after the camera finds a defect falls under the Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00, and the Water and Sewer Department can explain the town's rules for connections.

How to tell it is the main line

One slow fixture is a branch clog. When the tub gurgles as the toilet flushes, the basement floor drain bubbles or several drains slow together, the main line is the likely culprit. A cleanout outside with standing water confirms it.

A main line clog tends to build up over time and then stops suddenly, often after laundry or a full bath adds load to the system.

Roots, grease and what else blocks a main

Roots enter at joints seeking water and nutrients, and they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cast iron, clay and early plastic joints are the vulnerable ones. A cutting head clears the roots, but they grow back unless the joint is repaired or the pipe is lined.

Wipes sold as flushable do not break down the way paper does. They bunch around roots and bellies and form the plug. Cooking grease poured down the drain hardens further down and does the same.

Cabling, jetting and the camera

A cable machine with a cutting head clears the plug. Hydro jetting then scours the pipe wall. A camera run confirms the result and shows any cracks, offsets or bellies that would bring the clog back.

We recommend the camera for any main that clogs more than once, because a clean pipe with a defect will clog again.

Who is responsible for the sewer connection

Responsibility for the sewer line usually changes at the property line or the connection to the town main. Ask the Marblehead Water and Sewer Department where the boundary falls for your address, since a blockage in the town main is the town's to clear.
