Emergency hydro jetting in Marblehead, MA

A main drain that backs up again soon after it was snaked is often a hydro jetting job, and our line is answered 24/7. Stop using water in the house, and call. Same-day visits are booked when a slot is available.

Hydro jetting uses a hose and a nozzle that fires high-pressure water jets against the pipe wall as it moves through the line. It removes grease, scale, sludge and fine roots that a cable leaves behind. We pair it with a camera so you can see the pipe before and after.

Emergency: main line backups and what to do first

A main line backup can push wastewater into a basement within minutes. The first rule is to stop adding water, so every sink, shower and appliance should be off.

Jetting on a rocky peninsula

Marblehead's granite ledges make deep digging a slow and costly job, which is why we try to clear and clean a line before recommending excavation. Jetting on a sound line can postpone a dig or avoid one. It does not fix a broken pipe, and the camera tells us which situation we are in.

Homes in Old Town and Clifton range widely in age, so drain material varies. We use camera checks to judge each line before we apply pressure to it.

Jetting equipment is used according to the manufacturer's guidelines and the plumber's judgment of the pipe, which is why the camera comes first. Any pipe repair that follows is held to the Uniform State Plumbing Code, 248 CMR 10.00.

Why a snake keeps failing

A cable cuts a hole through the blockage and leaves the rest of the coating on the pipe wall. Water drains for a while, then the line narrows again. That pattern, clear for a few weeks and then back, is the main reason homeowners are advised to jet.

Jetting does not just open the line. It cleans a full circle of pipe wall, which is why it lasts longer on lines clogged by grease and scale. It also washes sediment forward to the municipal connection instead of leaving it pooled in a belly.

What jetting will not do

Jetting is not a repair. A cracked, collapsed or offset pipe can be damaged further by high pressure, so we run a camera first and decline to jet a line that is not sound. A heavy root mass may need a cutting head first, then a jet to finish the job.

If the camera shows a defect, the plumber explains whether sewer line repair is the real answer and why.

Where jetting fits in a Marblehead home

Restaurants, boarding houses and large older homes with long drain runs tend to build grease and scale. The same is true for homes that rely on an old cast iron main. Jetting suits those lines when the pipe is sound enough to take it.

Layouts on Marblehead Neck and in Old Town can differ a lot from house to house. We ask about the layout on the call, so we bring the right hose length.

Preparing for a jetting visit

  • Locate your cleanout, usually in the basement or at the foundation.
  • Clear a path to the cleanout and move items away from the floor drain.
  • Tell the plumber which fixtures backed up and when.
  • Do not run water in the house while the line is being worked.
