Where we work in Peabody: the corridors

Route 114 corridor

Route 114 links Marblehead, Salem and Peabody, and it is the road a crew takes. Homes along it range from older houses near the Salem line to later subdivisions. Calls are a mix of water heaters, drains and leaks.

Route 128 corridor

The Route 128 side has newer commercial buildings, offices and shops, so commercial calls increase. See commercial plumbing for after-hours work.

Residential streets

Residential Peabody mixes older and newer homes, so one street can have galvanized pipe and PEX side by side. A plumber should look at each house on its own terms.

Peabody's water: local sources and the MWRA

Peabody's own water comes mainly from the Walsh and Winona treatment plants. The city's local sources are Winona Pond, Suntaug Lake and Spring Pond, and the MWRA supplies supplemental water during high-demand months. According to the city's 2023 letter, the MWRA provided 22 percent of supplemental supply that year.

Local withdrawals from the Ipswich River are allowed only in winter and spring under state rules, so the city asks residents to conserve during dry stretches. For a house, that mostly means using water-saving fixtures and fixing leaks quickly. See leak detection if a meter is moving.

Lead goosenecks and service line replacement

The same 2023 letter reports 90th-percentile lead results of 1.73 and 2.14 parts per billion across its sampling, below the 15 ppb action level. It also says the city removed 38 lead goosenecks through its Lead Service Line Replacement Program.

If your home is older, ask the Peabody Public Services Department about your service line. For the line from the street to your meter, see water service line repair.

While the plumber travels to Peabody

The drive is about 8 miles, so a few minutes of preparation matters. These steps limit the damage.

  • Water: Close the main shutoff.
  • Power: Switch off breakers to wet rooms.
  • Drains: Stop water use if a drain is backing up.
  • Access: Clear a path to the heater, meter or cleanout.

Drains and sewers

Mixed housing ages mean mixed drain materials. Cast iron on older streets, plastic on newer ones. Root intrusion is common where mature trees sit near the lateral. Drain cleaning clears the clog and hydro jetting cleans the line. A camera inspection shows what is there.

Winter and storm calls in Peabody

Peabody sits inland from the coast, but winter still freezes exterior pipes, hose bibs and unheated garages. A faucet that drips on the coldest nights keeps water moving, and a pipe that has frozen once should be checked after it thaws. See burst pipe repair for what a plumber does first.

Spring thaw and heavy rain fill sump pits quickly. If a pump runs constantly or not at all, call early and see sump pump repair.

Commercial and rental properties near Route 128

Shops, offices and small rental buildings need response windows that fit their hours. We schedule around business hours when we can, and the 24/7 line covers emergencies. A written note of the finding and the repair helps with inspectors and landlords.
