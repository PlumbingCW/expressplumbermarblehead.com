Who Supplies Water And Handles Sewer In Each Town

Questions about a street main, a meter or a public sewer go to the town or utility that runs it, not to a plumber. These notes come from each utility's own published information, and they tell you who to call before any digging starts.

Marblehead

The town buys all of its water from the MWRA, and its Water and Sewer Department maintains the sewer, which is pumped to the South Essex Sewerage District plant in Salem.

Swampscott

Swampscott buys all of its water from the MWRA, and the town Department of Public Works runs the local distribution system.

Salem

The Salem and Beverly Water Supply Board supplies and treats the water, and the City of Salem runs the local system.

Lynn

The Lynn Water and Sewer Commission supplies water from its own sources and also takes away wastewater.

Peabody

Peabody draws mainly on its Walsh and Winona treatment plants, with the MWRA supplying supplemental water in high-demand months.
